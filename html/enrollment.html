📌 Status Guide

This allows you to update the enrollment status (e.g., Normal, Repeating, Promoted, or Graduated) for the selected student(s).

Note: Select the student row using the checkbox first before clicking this button.

📌 Semester Move Guide

This transitions the selected student(s) directly into the 2nd Semester for their current class and academic year, creating their new semester fee structure records.

Note: Use this when students are staying in the same grade/class for the next semester rather than changing grade levels.

📌 Promotion Guide

This will promote this student to the next class and 2nd semester with the fee structure.

Note: Does not apply to students coming from classes below Grade 9 going to Grade 10+. Classes 10 and above require a user account—please use the Promote Grade 9 to 10 (Create User) button instead.

📌 New Year Movement Guide

Transfers or shifts students to a different class section or stream for the new academic year.

Note: Use for lateral reassignments or class section adjustments without grade progression.

📌 New Year Promotion Guide

Advances students from their current grade level to the next sequential grade for the upcoming academic year.

Note: Standard grade-to-grade progression (e.g., Grade 7 to 8, Grade 8 to 9).

📌 Grade 9 to 10 Promotion Guide

This will promote this student to the next class and 2nd semester with the fee structure.

Note: Does not apply to students coming from classes below Grade 9 going to Grade 10+. Classes 10 and above require a user account—please use this button instead.
